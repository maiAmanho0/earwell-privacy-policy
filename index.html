EarWell Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

EarWell respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through our Meta Lead Ads forms is collected, used, processed and protected in connection with our EarWell lead-generation demonstration.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an EarWell lead form, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • City
  • Selected ear-related concern, such as ear wax, ringing, hearing difficulty or general ear care
  • Whether the enquiry is for yourself or a family member
  • Lead submission and processing timestamps

2. How We Use Your Information

We use submitted information to:

  • Receive and manage enquiries about EarWell.
  • Classify enquiries according to their stated requirements.
  • Contact people about their enquiries and requested information.
  • Notify the relevant sales or support team about new enquiries.
  • Maintain lead records and prevent duplicate entries.
  • Test and improve the lead-management workflow.

3. How Information Is Processed

Information submitted through Meta Lead Ads may be processed using the following services as part of the demonstration:

  • Meta: Lead form submission and lead delivery.
  • n8n: Workflow automation and lead processing.
  • Google Sheets: Lead record storage.
  • Gmail: Internal email notifications.

These services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data Sharing

Information may be accessed by authorised people responsible for handling enquiries and by the service providers listed above where necessary to operate the workflow. We do not share information for unrelated purposes.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for enquiry management, operational purposes and demonstration testing. Test records may be deleted when they are no longer required.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit access to lead information and protect it against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your submitted information. You may also ask us to stop contacting you about your enquiry.

8. Children's Privacy

This demonstration is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our data-processing practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
